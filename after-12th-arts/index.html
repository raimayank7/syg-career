What to do after Class 12 Arts

Arts is not the stream with fewer options. It is the stream where the options are less advertised.

Degrees

  • BA in a subject you want to go deep into: economics, psychology, political science, history, sociology, geography, English or another language. Central universities admit through CUET.
  • Integrated law, five years, through CLAT and other tests.
  • Design: B.Des through NID, UCEED or NIFT tests. Any stream may apply.
  • Journalism and mass communication.
  • Hotel management, through NCHM JEE for the national institutes.
  • Social work (BSW), fine arts, performing arts and physical education.
  • Teaching: D.El.Ed after 12th, or the four year integrated teacher education course.

Where a BA leads

A BA is a starting point, not an end. Psychology leads to counselling and human resources after a master's. Economics leads to analysis, banking and policy. Languages lead to translation, teaching and content work. Political science, history and geography are popular bases for the civil services.

Government jobs

After 12th: SSC CHSL for clerical posts, constable posts, the defence forces through NDA (Army wing) and Agniveer, and railway posts. After graduation: SSC CGL, banks, state services and UPSC.

Make the three years count

Arts graduates who do well usually add something to the degree: strong writing in English or their own language, a second language, computer skills, internships, or a clear plan for a master's. Start on one of these in the first year.

Questions people ask

Can an arts student become a lawyer?

Yes. Five year law courses admit students of any stream through CLAT and other tests.

Can arts students apply for the defence forces?

Yes, for the Army wing of the NDA and for Agniveer entries that accept any stream. The Air Force and Navy wings of the NDA need Physics and Mathematics.
