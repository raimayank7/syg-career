Bank jobs: the path

Public sector banks recruit graduates of any subject through common examinations. The selection is fast compared with most government jobs.

What the work is

Clerical staff handle customers and counters. Probationary officers train across the branch and move into management: loans, operations, branch head. Transfers are part of an officer's life.

The path, step by step

  1. A degree in any subject.
  2. Apply to the examination: IBPS holds common recruitment for most public sector banks (PO, clerk, specialist officer) and for regional rural banks. State Bank of India holds its own.
  3. Preliminary and Main examinations on computer: reasoning, quantitative aptitude, English, general and banking awareness. Officers also face an interview.
  4. Allotment to a bank, then training and probation.

Examinations and bodies

Examination or bodyWhat it is for
IBPS PO and SBI POFor probationary officers. The usual age range is 20 to 30, with relaxation for some categories.
IBPS Clerk and SBI ClerkFor clerical posts, usually 20 to 28.
IBPS RRBFor regional rural banks, which serve rural areas within a state.
RBI Grade B and AssistantThe Reserve Bank's own recruitment.

How long it takes

A recruitment cycle takes a few months from examination to joining.

Honest notes

  • The examinations test speed. Regular timed practice matters more than long study hours.
  • Officers are transferred every few years, often to small towns. Clerks usually stay within a state.
  • Sales targets for insurance, loans and accounts are a real part of the job today.
  • Prepare the photo, signature and declaration files exactly as the notice asks, or the form will not go through.

Is this path for you?

It may suit you if:

  • You are careful with figures and with other people's money.
  • You can deal politely with the public all day.
  • You are willing to be posted to a small town or village.

Think again if:

  • You want to stay in one city for good.
  • You dislike targets. Bank work today includes selling products, not only keeping accounts.

If the main road does not open

The same preparation in reasoning, arithmetic and English serves insurance companies, SSC, railways and state recruitment. Private banks, small finance banks and finance companies hire directly, without a common examination. Courses in accounts, insurance or investment add to what you can offer.
