Resume questions and answers

Short answers to the questions job seekers in India ask most, with links to the full guides.

Writing the resume

How long should my resume be?

One page if you are a fresher or have less than about ten years of experience. Two pages at most for longer careers.

What is the best resume format for freshers?

Name and contact, a short objective, education, projects and internships, skills, achievements, languages. The builder arranges these when you choose Fresher.

What should I write in the objective?

Two or three lines: what you have studied or practised, what you can do, and the role you want. Use your own facts, not a copied sentence.

Do I need numbers in my resume?

They make your points believable. Say how many, how much or how often wherever you honestly can, and use "about" for estimates.

Should I list hobbies?

Only in one line and only if they say something relevant or memorable. They are optional.

Can I use the same resume for every job?

Keep a master copy and adjust the headline, summary, skills and order of points for each application.

Indian practice

Should I add a photo?

Only when the employer asks. Most private employers do not need one.

Should I add date of birth, father's name or marital status?

Not on a resume for a private job, unless the application asks. Forms that need these details have their own fields.

Is a declaration with signature needed?

No, unless the employer or institution specifically asks for it.

What is the difference between a resume, a CV and a biodata?

A resume is a short document for one job; a CV is a full career record; a biodata lists personal particulars. Most private employers want a resume.

How do I show years spent preparing for competitive exams?

In one honest line in date order: the period, the examinations, any stage reached, and the skills you built.

Should I mention my current or expected salary?

No. Discuss pay when the employer raises it.

Sending and after

What file name should I use?

Your name and the word resume, such as Neha_Kulkarni_Resume.pdf.

What do I write in the email?

A subject naming the post, then three or four lines: the post, two or three facts that match it, and that your resume is attached.

How soon should I follow up?

Once, after a week to ten days, if you have a contact. Then keep applying elsewhere.

Someone is asking for money to get me a job. Is that normal?

No. Genuine employers do not charge candidates. Treat it as a warning sign and do not pay.
