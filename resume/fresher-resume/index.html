Fresher resume format: what to write when you have no experience

You have more to write than you think. Experience is not only paid work: it is anything you did that shows you can do the job.

The order for a fresher

  1. Name, contact and headline
  2. Career objective (two or three lines)
  3. Education
  4. Projects and internships
  5. Any work experience, including part-time
  6. Skills
  7. Achievements and certificates
  8. Languages

Education comes first because it is your strongest fact. In the builder, choose "Fresher" and the sections arrange themselves in this order.

What counts as experience

Employers hiring freshers do not expect a job history. They look for signs that you can turn up, learn and finish things. All of these count, if described honestly:

  • Internships and in-plant training, however short.
  • College projects, especially the final year project.
  • Part-time and weekend work: shops, tuition, delivery, call centres.
  • Helping in a family business: billing, accounts, stock, customers.
  • Positions of responsibility: class representative, club treasurer, event coordinator, NCC or NSS.
  • Freelance or personal work: a website for a relative's shop, a YouTube channel you edit, designs for a college fest.
  • Certificate courses with practical work.

Turning it into resume lines

For each item, ask four questions. What did I actually do? How much or how many? With what tool? What came of it?

Weak

Did internship in a company and learned many things about accounts.

Better

Entered about 40 purchase and sales vouchers a day in Tally for six trading clients and matched bank statements with the cash book.

Weak

Was a member of the cultural committee.

Better

Managed registrations for the annual fest: 450 participants, ₹60,000 in fees collected and accounted for.

The objective

Write it last. Two or three lines: what you have studied or practised, what you can do on day one, and the kind of role you want. Avoid the copied sentence about "a challenging position in a reputed organisation where I can utilise my skills". Thousands of resumes carry it and recruiters no longer read it. Examples for many fields are on the summary and objective page.

Marks

Many fresher recruiters shortlist on marks, so state your degree score and, for campus-type hiring, Class 12 and Class 10 as well. Give the figure in the form on your marksheet, CGPA or percentage. If a score is weak, you may leave out that one figure unless the advertisement asks for it, but never alter it. More on the education section page.

Skills a fresher can honestly claim

List what you can demonstrate if asked. "MS Excel (formulas, sorting, simple charts)" is honest and specific. "Expert in Excel" from a fresher invites a test. Typing speed, spoken languages and the software used in your projects all belong here. See skills.

Mistakes freshers make

  • Filling space with a long declaration, personal details and hobbies while saying almost nothing about what they can do.
  • Sending the same resume for a sales job and an accounts job.
  • Listing every subject studied.
  • Stretching to two pages with large fonts.
  • An email address like coolboy2004@. Make a plain one with your name.
  • Typing and grammar errors. Have someone read it.

Questions people ask

What do I write in a resume with no experience at all?

Education, a college project, any course with practical work, positions of responsibility, and help given in a family business or community, each described by what you actually did.

Should a fresher write an objective or a summary?

An objective of two or three lines: what you have learned, what you can do and the role you want.

Is it all right to leave out low marks?

You may leave out a figure the advertisement does not ask for. Never change a figure, and be ready to state it truthfully if asked.
