Sales executive resume sample (3 years of experience)

For field sales, area sales and business development executives looking for a better role. In sales, the resume is judged on one thing: numbers against target. Put them where they cannot be missed.

Every name, employer and institution in this sample is invented. In the builder it is laid out on one A4 page; replace each line with your own facts.

Why it is written this way

  • The headline gives role, industry and years: the three filters a recruiter applies.
  • The summary is a list of results, each with a number: outlets, months on target, new accounts.
  • Points begin with action words and end with an outcome.
  • Rank among peers ("second among nine") gives the numbers meaning.
  • Education is brief and placed after experience, as it should be for someone with a work record.

What to change for yourself

  • Use your own target and achievement figures. If your employer treats sales values as confidential, use percentages and counts.
  • Name the product category you sold; it matters when changing industry.
  • If you managed people or a larger territory, bring that to the first point.
  • Keep the reason for leaving out of the resume.

What to avoid

  • Duties without results: "responsible for achieving targets".
  • Rounding everything to suspiciously perfect figures.
  • Listing every scheme and product. Choose the strongest five or six points.

Questions people ask

Should I mention my current salary?

No. A resume does not carry current or expected pay. Share it when the recruiter asks.

Can I write approximate numbers?

Yes, if they are honest estimates you can explain. Use words such as "about" where the figure is not exact.
