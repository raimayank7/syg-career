MBA marketing fresher resume sample

For management graduates applying for marketing, sales, brand and business development trainee roles. Recruiters at this level look for evidence that you can talk to customers, handle numbers and take responsibility.

Open this sample in the builder

The sample

Simran Kaur Gill

MBA (Marketing) 2026 | Sales and Market Research

98XXX XXX10 | simran@example.com | Chandigarh

Career objective

MBA student with a two month summer internship in channel sales, where I visited 120 retail outlets and added 18 new ones for a packaged food distributor. Enjoy field work and working with data in Excel. Seeking a management trainee role in sales or marketing.

Education

MBA, Marketing and Analytics, Institute of Management, Chandigarh, 2024 to 2026, 7.9 CGPA

B.Com, A university college, Ludhiana, 2021 to 2024, 76%

Class 12, Commerce, CBSE, 2021, 89%

Projects and internships

Summer Intern, Channel Sales, A packaged food distributor, Ludhiana (May to Jun 2025)

  • Visited 120 general stores across four areas of the city and recorded stock, display and competitor prices
  • Added 18 new outlets, with first orders worth about ₹2.1 lakh in total
  • Built an Excel sheet ranking outlets by monthly offtake, now used by two salesmen to plan beats
  • Presented five suggestions to the owner; two (a combo pack and a display stand) were tried

Live Project: Customer Survey for a Local Bakery, Course project, team of four (Sep to Oct 2025)

  • Designed a 12 question survey and collected 210 responses at the outlet and online
  • Found that evening stock-outs were the main complaint; the owner extended baking hours

Skills

Sales and research: retail visits, surveys, competitor price tracking, basic negotiation

Tools: MS Excel (pivot tables, charts), PowerPoint, Google Forms, Canva

Digital: basics of social media pages and search advertising (course level)

Achievements and certificates

  • Head of the Marketing Club, 2025-26: organised a sales contest with 14 teams and sponsorship of ₹40,000
  • Second prize, inter-college case study competition, 2025

Additional information

Languages: English, Punjabi, Hindi

Every name, employer and institution in this sample is invented. In the builder it is laid out on one A4 page; replace each line with your own facts.

Why it is written this way

  • The summary opens with the strongest fact: outlets visited and added. Field numbers are what sales recruiters trust.
  • The internship reads as results, not duties. "Added 18 new outlets" is a result; "responsible for visiting outlets" is a duty.
  • The live project shows the full chain: question, data, finding, action taken by the client.
  • The club role includes money raised and teams handled, which shows responsibility on a small scale.
  • Digital skills are marked "course level", which is honest and still useful.

What to change for yourself

  • Use your own internship figures. If you do not have exact numbers, give careful approximations and be ready to explain how you arrived at them.
  • For a finance or HR specialisation, keep the structure and change the content: the pattern is the same.
  • If you worked before the MBA, switch the builder to "Experienced" and put that job first.
  • Mention amounts only if you are free to share them; otherwise use counts.

What to avoid

  • Listing every subject and every certificate from online courses.
  • Vague lines such as "gained exposure to the FMCG sector".
  • A two page resume padded with school-level achievements.

Questions people ask

Is a summer internship enough experience for an MBA fresher?

Yes, if it is described with what you did and what came of it. Add live projects and positions of responsibility to complete the picture.

Should I mention my graduation stream?

Yes. List the degree with its year and score; a different stream is often an advantage, not a weakness.
