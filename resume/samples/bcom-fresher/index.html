B.Com fresher resume sample (accounts assistant)

For commerce graduates applying for a first job as an accounts assistant, junior accountant, billing executive or article assistant. The sample shows how to make college work, a short internship and computer skills carry a resume that has no job history yet.

The sample

Neha Kulkarni

B.Com Graduate | Accounts and GST

98XXX XXX10 | neha@example.com | Pune, Maharashtra

Career objective

Commerce graduate with a two month internship at a CA firm, where I entered purchase and sales vouchers in Tally and helped prepare GST return data for small traders. Looking for an accounts assistant role where I can handle day to day entries and learn finalisation.

Education

B.Com, City College of Commerce, Pune, 2023 to 2026, 8.1 CGPA

Class 12, Commerce, State Board, 2023, 84%

Class 10, State Board, 2021, 88%

Projects and internships

Accounts Intern, A chartered accountant firm, Pune (May to Jun 2025)

  • Entered about 40 purchase and sales vouchers a day in Tally for six trading clients
  • Matched bank statements with the cash book and listed unreconciled items for the senior
  • Prepared sales and purchase summaries in Excel for monthly GST returns
  • Filed and indexed client bills, cutting the time to find a document during audit

College Project: Working Capital of a Small Retailer, Final year project, team of three (Jan to Mar 2026)

  • Collected two years of figures from a local garment shop and calculated liquidity and turnover ratios
  • Presented findings to a panel of two faculty members

Skills

Accounting: Tally (vouchers, ledgers, GST reports), bank reconciliation, journal entries

Computer: MS Excel (SUMIF, VLOOKUP, pivot tables), MS Word, email

Other: English typing 35 words a minute

Achievements and certificates

  • Second rank in the college in B.Com second year
  • Completed a 60 hour certificate course in Tally with GST, 2025
  • Treasurer of the Commerce Association, 2025-26: kept the event accounts

Additional information

Languages: English, Hindi, Marathi

Every name, employer and institution in this sample is invented. In the builder it is laid out on one A4 page; replace each line with your own facts.

Why it is written this way

  • The headline says in six words what she is and what she can do, so a recruiter knows the fit before reading further.
  • The objective names real work done (vouchers, GST data) instead of adjectives such as "hardworking".
  • The internship is written like a job: action word first, then a number wherever one exists.
  • Skills are grouped and specific. "Excel (VLOOKUP, pivot tables)" can be tested in an interview and tells the employer the level.
  • The treasurer role is included because it is about handling money, which is relevant. A dance prize would not be.

What to change for yourself

  • Replace the internship with whatever practical exposure you have: helping in a family shop's accounts, a part-time billing job, or a longer course project.
  • If your marks are modest, give the degree and year and leave the score out unless the advertisement asks for it.
  • List only software you have actually used. You will be asked to show it.
  • If you have cleared any professional examination stage, put it at the top of Education.

What to avoid

  • Writing "Tally" with no detail. Say what you did in it.
  • Listing every subject of B.Com. The employer knows the syllabus.
  • Adding a long declaration, father's name and date of birth by habit. See what is needed on the personal details page.

Questions people ask

I have no internship. What do I write?

Use a college project, a certificate course with practical work, or unpaid help in a relative's business, described honestly with what you actually did.

Should a B.Com fresher's resume be one page?

Yes. With no job history, one page is enough and is easier for a recruiter to read.
