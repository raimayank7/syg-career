Customer support (BPO) fresher resume sample

For graduates and undergraduates of any stream applying for voice, chat or email support and back-office roles. These employers hire on clear speech, typing, patience and reliability, so the resume must show those with evidence.

Open this sample in the builder

The sample

Ritika Sharma

B.A. Graduate | Customer Support (Voice and Chat)

98XXX XXX10 | ritika@example.com | Jaipur, Rajasthan

Career objective

Arts graduate with clear spoken English and Hindi and a typing speed of 40 words a minute. Handled the help desk at two college festivals and worked part time at a mobile store, answering customer questions and billing. Comfortable with rotational shifts.

Education

B.A. (English, Sociology), A government college, Jaipur, 2023 to 2026, 68%

Class 12, Arts, State Board, 2023, 74%

Projects and internships

Help Desk Volunteer, College annual festival (Feb 2025 and Feb 2026)

  • Answered queries of about 300 visitors a day on events, timings and registration
  • Logged lost-and-found items in a shared sheet; 22 of 30 items were returned

Work experience

Part-time Sales Assistant, A mobile phone store, Jaipur (Nov 2024 to Feb 2026 (weekends))

  • Attended 25 to 30 walk-in customers a day: explained plans, accessories and warranty terms
  • Prepared bills on the store software and recorded exchanges and returns
  • Calmed and resolved customer complaints about delayed repairs by following up with the service centre

Skills

Communication: spoken and written English and Hindi, telephone etiquette, active listening

Computer: typing 40 wpm, MS Excel (data entry, filters), MS Word, email, internet search

Other: billing software, handling cash and UPI payments

Achievements and certificates

  • Winner, college debate competition, 2025
  • Completed a 30 hour spoken English and soft skills course, 2024

Additional information

Languages: English, Hindi, Rajasthani

Every name, employer and institution in this sample is invented. In the builder it is laid out on one A4 page; replace each line with your own facts.

Why it is written this way

  • The summary gives measurable facts the recruiter screens for: languages, typing speed, shift readiness.
  • The part-time job is real customer contact, so it goes under work experience even though it was weekends.
  • The complaint-handling point shows patience, which is the heart of support work.
  • Volunteer work is described with numbers of people handled.
  • Nothing is exaggerated; every line could be checked in a two minute conversation.

What to change for yourself

  • Test your own typing speed and write the true figure.
  • Add any regional or foreign language you can hold a conversation in; it can open specific processes.
  • If you prefer day shifts or a particular city, do not write it here; raise it in the interview.
  • Replace the store job with any work where you dealt with the public: tuition, reception, a family shop.

What to avoid

  • Calling yourself a "fluent" speaker of a language you only understand.
  • Long objective statements copied from the internet.
  • Leaving the phone number with a mistake: this is the commonest reason a call never comes.

Questions people ask

Can I apply for support jobs without a degree?

Many employers accept Class 12 or undergraduate candidates for some roles. Read the advertisement; if you are still studying, state the course and expected year.

Do I need to mention shift preference?

Say only that you are comfortable with rotational shifts if that is true. Otherwise leave it out and discuss it later.
