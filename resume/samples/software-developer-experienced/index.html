Software developer resume sample (4 years of experience)

For developers changing jobs after three to six years. At this stage reviewers look for ownership: what you designed, what you improved and by how much, more than the list of technologies.

The sample

98XXX XXX10 | ananya@example.com | Bengaluru, Karnataka | github.com/yourname

Every name, employer and institution in this sample is invented. In the builder it is laid out on one A4 page; replace each line with your own facts.

Why it is written this way

  • Every point pairs an action with an effect, in figures: requests per day, milliseconds, cases a month.
  • "Own" and "designed" show responsibility beyond writing code to order.
  • The skill list is short and grouped, with levels where they differ.
  • Mentoring and on-call appear, because senior roles ask for them.
  • Company names are less important than what was built; the points carry the weight.

What to change for yourself

  • Use your own metrics. If you do not have exact figures, give honest ranges and explain how they were measured if asked.
  • Do not disclose anything covered by your employer's confidentiality rules; describe the system in general terms.
  • Lead with the technologies the target job asks for, if you truly know them.
  • With more than one notable project per job, keep the best three or four points.

What to avoid

  • A wall of technology names.
  • Words such as "passionate" and "ninja".
  • Describing the product instead of your part in it.

Questions people ask

Should I list every technology I have touched?

No. List what you could discuss in depth. A focused list is more convincing.

Do I need a summary with four years of experience?

A three line summary helps the reader place you quickly. Keep it factual.
