School teacher resume sample

For primary, TGT and PGT teachers applying to private schools. Schools look for qualification, classes and subjects taught, class results, command of language and what you add outside the classroom.

Every name, employer and institution in this sample is invented. In the builder it is laid out on one A4 page; replace each line with your own facts.

Why it is written this way

  • Qualifications sit in the headline: schools screen on B.Ed. and eligibility tests first.
  • Results are given as facts about the class, without boasting.
  • Duties beyond teaching (class teacher, exhibition) show a full member of staff.
  • The summary describes a way of teaching in one line, which gives the interview panel something to ask about.
  • Languages are listed, as medium of instruction matters.

What to change for yourself

  • State the board and the classes you have taught.
  • If you are a fresher, replace jobs with teaching practice during B.Ed.: school, classes, number of lessons and what your supervisor noted.
  • Add any eligibility test you have qualified, with year.
  • For primary posts, bring out activities, handwriting, storytelling and classroom management.

What to avoid

  • A long list of every seminar attended.
  • Emotional lines about loving children; show it through what you do.
  • Leaving out the subjects and classes.

Questions people ask

I am a fresher with only teaching practice. Is that enough?

Yes. Describe the internship school, classes taught, number of lessons and any teaching aids you made.

Should a teacher's resume include a photo?

Only if the school asks for one. Otherwise it is not needed.
