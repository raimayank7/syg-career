HR executive resume sample (2 years of experience)

For HR executives and recruiters early in their careers. Since HR staff read resumes all day, yours is a sample of your own work: it must be exact, tidy and free of the clichés you reject in others.

Open this sample in the builder

The sample

Fatima Khan

HR Executive | Recruitment and Onboarding | 2 Years

98XXX XXX10 | fatima@example.com | Noida, Uttar Pradesh

Summary

HR executive with two years of experience in end to end recruitment for a 300 person services company. Closed 85 positions in the last year with an average time to fill of 24 days. Also handle onboarding, attendance and payroll inputs. Looking for an HR generalist role.

Work experience

HR Executive, An IT-enabled services company, Noida (Jul 2024 to present)

  • Recruit for support, sales and junior technical roles: closed 85 positions in 12 months
  • Reduced average time to fill from 35 days to 24 by building a referral scheme and a shortlist bank
  • Conduct first-round interviews, about 15 a week, and coordinate panels for later rounds
  • Run joining formalities and a one day induction for 6 to 10 new employees every month
  • Send attendance and leave data to payroll by the 25th; resolved about 20 employee queries a month

HR Intern, A manufacturing company, Ghaziabad (May to Jun 2023)

  • Updated 400 employee records in the HR system and found 35 with missing documents
  • Helped organise a safety week for 250 workers

Skills

Recruitment: sourcing from job portals and referrals, screening, interviewing, offer follow-up

HR operations: onboarding, attendance and leave records, payroll inputs, employee queries

Tools: job portals, HR software, MS Excel (pivot tables, VLOOKUP), video interview tools

Education

MBA (Human Resources), An institute of management, Ghaziabad, 2022 to 2024, 7.6 CGPA

B.A. (Psychology), A university college, Delhi, 2019 to 2022, 69%

Achievements and certificates

  • Employee of the Month, March 2025
  • Certificate in labour law basics (online course), 2024

Additional information

Languages: English, Hindi, Urdu

Every name, employer and institution in this sample is invented. In the builder it is laid out on one A4 page; replace each line with your own facts.

Why it is written this way

  • Recruitment is described with the two figures every HR head asks for: positions closed and time to fill.
  • The improvement has a cause attached (referral scheme, shortlist bank), which makes it believable.
  • Routine work is given volume: interviews a week, joiners a month, queries resolved.
  • The move towards a generalist role is supported by onboarding and payroll points.
  • The page is plain and exact, which is itself a recommendation for an HR person.

What to change for yourself

  • Use your own hiring numbers and the kinds of roles you fill.
  • If you handle statutory work such as provident fund or state insurance filings, name it under HR operations.
  • For a recruitment consultancy background, show clients served and roles by level.
  • Do not name candidates or disclose confidential pay data.

What to avoid

  • "People person" and similar phrases.
  • Listing HR theory subjects from the MBA.
  • Typing errors. In an HR resume they are fatal.

Questions people ask

How do I move from recruitment to a generalist role?

Bring out every non-recruitment task you already do: onboarding, records, payroll inputs, employee queries, events. Ask for more of these in your present job and add them.

Should I mention the job portals I use?

It is enough to say job portals and referrals. Name a tool only if the advertisement asks for it.
