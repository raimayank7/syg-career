Resume sample after Class 12 (office assistant, data entry)

For candidates who have completed Class 12, or are studying by distance mode, and want office assistant, data entry operator, receptionist or store helper jobs. A short, clean page that shows typing, computer basics and dependability is all that is needed.

The sample

Pooja Yadav

Class 12 Pass | Data Entry and Office Work

98XXX XXX10 | pooja@example.com | Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh

Career objective

Class 12 pass with a six month computer course and typing speed of 35 words a minute in English and 25 in Hindi. Helped my uncle's transport office with entries and filing for a year. Looking for a full-time data entry or office assistant job.

Education

B.A. (first year, distance mode), An open university, 2025 onwards, Studying

Class 12, Arts, State Board, 2025, 71%

Office Helper (part time), A transport booking office, Lucknow (Apr 2024 to Mar 2025)

  • Entered 30 to 40 booking slips a day into an Excel register
  • Filed delivery receipts by date and party, so that any receipt could be found in under two minutes
  • Answered the office phone and noted messages for the owner

Office: filing, phone handling, photocopying, courier and stock registers

Additional information

Languages: Hindi, English

Every name, employer and institution in this sample is invented. In the builder it is laid out on one A4 page; replace each line with your own facts.

Why it is written this way

  • It is short. For entry-level office jobs, half a page of true facts is better than a full page of padding.
  • Typing speed in two languages is stated first because data entry tests begin with a typing test.
  • Unpaid family work is presented as experience, with the tasks done.
  • The degree in progress is shown honestly as "studying".
  • Skills are everyday office tasks the employer needs done from day one.

What to change for yourself

  • Take a free online typing test and write your real speed.
  • List the exact computer course and its duration.
  • If you can use a two-wheeler for errands and the job needs it, mention a valid licence.
  • Add any shop, tuition or household business work you have done regularly.

What to avoid

  • Leaving the page almost empty. Add true details of what you can do.
  • Spelling mistakes: ask someone to read the page before you send it.
  • Fancy fonts, borders and colours.

Questions people ask

Is it all right if my resume is only half a page?

Yes. Do not stretch it with large fonts or filler. A neat half page is fine for a first job.

Should I attach certificates with the resume?

Send them only if asked. Carry originals and photocopies to the interview.
