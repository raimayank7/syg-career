How to become a chartered accountant

CA is a professional course you can do alongside college, at low cost. What it costs instead is years of steady effort.

What the work is

Chartered accountants audit accounts, handle tax, and advise businesses on money. Company secretaries look after company law and governance. Cost accountants study what things cost to make and how to control it.

The path, step by step

  1. After Class 12 in any stream, register with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) and pass the Foundation examination. Graduates can enter directly at the Intermediate level.
  2. Pass the Intermediate examination.
  3. Do the practical training, called articleship, under a practising chartered accountant.
  4. Pass the Final examination and apply for membership of ICAI.

Examinations and bodies

Examination or bodyWhat it is for
ICAI examinationsFoundation, Intermediate and Final, held by ICAI itself. There is no separate entrance test by another body.
ICSIThe Institute of Company Secretaries of India runs the CS course, starting with CSEET.
ICMAIThe Institute of Cost Accountants of India runs the CMA course.

How long it takes

About four to five years after 12th if every stage is cleared at the first attempt. Many students take longer.

Honest notes

  • Pass percentages at each stage are low. Most people who finish have failed at least one attempt on the way. Plan for that, in time and in spirit.
  • Commerce in Class 11 and 12 helps but is not required.
  • Most students do a B.Com alongside, by regular or distance mode, so that they hold a degree whatever happens.
  • Articleship is real office work with long hours in the busy season. It is also where most of the learning happens.

Is this path for you?

It may suit you if:

  • You are comfortable with numbers and with rules that must be followed exactly.
  • You can study by yourself for long stretches. Much of this course is self-study.
  • You can face failing a paper and sitting it again.

Think again if:

  • You want a college life with fixed classes and long holidays.
  • You lose heart quickly when results are slow.

If the main road does not open

Many students clear the course in more attempts than they planned, and some stop midway. Doing B.Com alongside means you hold a degree whatever happens. The company secretary and cost accountant courses, an M.Com, an MBA in finance, and bank or government examinations are all open to someone who has studied this far.
