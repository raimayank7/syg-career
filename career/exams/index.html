How to use this table

  1. Find the row for the course or job you want.
  2. Note who holds the examination, and open that body's official website.
  3. Read the latest notice or information bulletin in full: eligibility, age, subjects, dates and fee.
  4. Fill in the form yourself, early, and keep a copy.

Three cautions

  • Look-alike websites. Use only the official address, which usually ends in .gov.in, .nic.in or .ac.in. Sites that charge a "registration fee" to apply on your behalf are not official.
  • State examinations. Most states have their own tests for engineering, nursing, polytechnic, teaching and state services. They are not in this table; your state's education department lists them.
  • Change. Examinations are merged, renamed and reshaped every few years. If a name here has changed, the body's website will say what replaced it.

Questions people ask

Which exams can I take after 12th?

JEE Main, NEET UG, CUET UG, CLAT, NDA, NATA, design tests such as UCEED and NID DAT, NCHM JEE, CA Foundation and SSC CHSL, depending on your stream.

Which exams need a degree?

UPSC Civil Services, SSC CGL, bank examinations, CDS, AFCAT, CAT and CUET PG are for graduates. GATE needs an engineering or science degree, or the later years of one.

Where do I find the real dates?

Only on the website of the body that holds the examination. Dates differ every year and are sometimes changed after the notice.
