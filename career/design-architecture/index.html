Design and architecture careers

These courses are open to students of any stream, except architecture, which needs mathematics. Selection is by aptitude tests, not by marks alone.

What the work is

Designers shape products, clothes, graphics, apps and spaces so that they work for people. Architects design buildings and see them through construction.

The path, step by step

  1. Design: Class 12 in any stream. Entrance tests include NID DAT (National Institute of Design), UCEED (B.Des at the IITs) and the NIFT entrance (fashion and related design). Most have a drawing or studio test and a portfolio or interview stage.
  2. B.Des is four years.
  3. Architecture: Class 12 with Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry as the Council of Architecture requires, then NATA or JEE Main Paper 2. B.Arch is five years, including practical training.
  4. Architects register with the Council of Architecture to practise.

Examinations and bodies

How long it takes

Four years for B.Des. Five years for B.Arch.

Honest notes

  • Drawing skill helps but the tests look for observation and ideas more than fine art.
  • Private design colleges can be expensive. Compare what the fee buys: studios, faculty, placements.
  • Starting pay in design and architecture is often modest. The work rewards those who truly enjoy it.
  • Build a portfolio of your own projects from the first year. Employers ask for it before they ask for marks.

Is this path for you?

It may suit you if:

  • You draw, make or arrange things without being asked to.
  • You can take criticism of your work and do it again.
  • You notice how things are used, not only how they look.

Think again if:

  • You like the idea of being creative but do not actually make things in your free time.
  • You want one right answer. Design problems have many.

If the main road does not open

The best known institutes admit few. Many universities and private colleges also teach design, and in this field your portfolio counts for more than the name on the degree. Short courses, a fine arts or applied arts degree, or learning on the job with a studio, printer, builder or tailor are real ways in. For architecture the degree and registration are required, so there the choice is which approved college.
