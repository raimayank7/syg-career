Joining the defence forces

There are two kinds of entry: as an officer, through examinations and the SSB interview, and in the ranks, mainly through the Agnipath scheme.

What the work is

Officers lead soldiers, sailors and air warriors, and later command units. Those in the ranks do the trade they are trained for: infantry, technical, clerical, medical support and many more.

The path, step by step

  1. Officer after 12th: the NDA and NA Examination of UPSC, then the SSB interview and medical tests. Army wing accepts any stream; Air Force and Navy need Physics and Mathematics.
  2. Officer after graduation: the CDS Examination of UPSC, AFCAT for the Air Force, and technical and other direct entries.
  3. Ranks: recruitment as an Agniveer under the Agnipath scheme, for four years, with a share retained afterwards.
  4. Medical fitness is tested for every entry, and standards are strict.

Examinations and bodies

Examination or bodyWhat it is for
NDA and NA ExaminationHeld by UPSC for unmarried men and women who have passed or are appearing in Class 12, within the age limits in the notice.
CDS ExaminationHeld by UPSC for graduates.
AFCATThe Air Force's own test for graduates.
Agniveer recruitmentA written test, physical tests and medical examination, held separately by each force.

How long it takes

NDA training is three years at the academy and one at the service academy. Agniveer service is four years.

Honest notes

  • The SSB interview tests the whole person over several days, and most candidates are not recommended. It can be tried again.
  • Check the eyesight, height and other medical standards before you spend years preparing.
  • Agniveer is a four year engagement. Read the terms about what happens after it, and plan your further study.
  • Paramilitary forces and state police recruit separately, through SSC and state boards.

Is this path for you?

It may suit you if:

  • You are physically active and can take discipline and routine.
  • You work well in a team and can follow orders as well as give them.
  • You and your family accept postings far from home, and the risk that comes with the uniform.

Think again if:

  • You have a medical condition that the services do not accept; check the published medical standards first.
  • You mainly want a secure job. There are safer ones.

If the main road does not open

Selection has several stages, and many good candidates are turned back at the interview board or the medical. Graduates get further chances through other entries. The paramilitary forces, state police, coast guard and merchant navy use similar strengths, and none of the preparation for fitness and general knowledge is wasted.

Where to check

  • joinindianarmy.nic.in, joinindiannavy.gov.in and the Air Force career site
