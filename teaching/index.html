How to become a teacher in India

To teach in a school you need two things besides your own education: a teaching qualification and a pass in an eligibility test.

What the work is

Teachers plan lessons, teach, assess children and deal with parents. Primary teachers teach all subjects to one class; secondary teachers teach their own subject to many classes.

The path, step by step

  1. Primary classes (1 to 5): Class 12, then a two year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed), or a four year integrated degree.
  2. Classes 6 and above: a degree in your subject, then a two year B.Ed. A four year integrated course after 12th combines the two.
  3. Pass a Teacher Eligibility Test: CTET (central) or your state's TET. Paper I is for classes 1 to 5 and Paper II for classes 6 to 8.
  4. Apply for teacher recruitment: central schools such as Kendriya Vidyalayas hold their own selection, and each state recruits for its schools.
  5. College teaching: a master's degree, then UGC NET or a PhD.

Examinations and bodies

Examination or bodyWhat it is for
CTETCentral Teacher Eligibility Test, held by CBSE.
State TETEach state's own eligibility test for its schools.
UGC NETFor eligibility as an assistant professor and for junior research fellowship.

How long it takes

Two years after 12th for D.El.Ed. Four years for the integrated degree. Five years by the degree plus B.Ed route.

Honest notes

  • Passing CTET or TET makes you eligible. It is not a job. Recruitment is a separate, competitive step.
  • Check that the B.Ed or D.El.Ed college is recognised by the National Council for Teacher Education.
  • Private school pay varies widely. Ask about pay scale and working hours before joining.
  • Good teachers are short in mathematics, science and English in most states.

Is this path for you?

It may suit you if:

  • You enjoy explaining something a third time, in a third way.
  • You like being with children or young people for most of the day.
  • You are steady: a class needs the same teacher every day.

Think again if:

  • You are choosing it because it looks like an easy job. It is not.
  • You dislike speaking in front of a room.

If the main road does not open

Government teaching posts come through eligibility tests and recruitment that can take time. Meanwhile private schools, coaching, tutoring, content writing for education companies and work with education charities all use the same training. A postgraduate degree with NET opens college teaching and research.

Where to check

  • ctet.nic.in
  • ncte.gov.in for recognised institutions
  • Your state's school education or teacher recruitment board
