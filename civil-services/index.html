Civil services: the path

The civil services are open to a graduate of any subject. The examination is the hard part: very few of those who apply are selected.

What the work is

Civil servants run the administration: districts, departments, police, revenue, foreign relations. An IAS officer may be a district collector in one posting and write policy in a ministry in the next.

The path, step by step

  1. A degree in any subject from a recognised university. You can apply in the final year.
  2. UPSC Civil Services Examination: Preliminary (objective), Main (written, descriptive) and Personality Test (interview).
  3. Rank and preference decide the service: IAS, IPS, IFS, IRS and others.
  4. Training at the academy, then the first posting.
  5. Each state's Public Service Commission holds a similar examination for state services.

Examinations and bodies

Examination or bodyWhat it is for
UPSC Civil Services ExaminationFor the all-India and central services. The general age range is 21 to 32, with relaxation for some categories, and attempts are limited.
State PSC examinationsFor state administrative, police and other services.

How long it takes

One examination cycle takes about a year. Most selected candidates needed more than one attempt.

Honest notes

  • Have a second plan. Most serious, capable candidates do not get in, simply because the seats are few.
  • Coaching is not compulsory. The syllabus and past papers are public.
  • Choose a degree you would be glad to work with anyway. It is your safety net and often your optional subject.
  • State services are a real career, not a consolation prize, and the work is closer to home.

Where to check

  • upsc.gov.in for the notice and syllabus
  • Your state Public Service Commission's website
